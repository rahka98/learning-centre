IMAGE REQUIREMENT GUIDE — Alagappa University Study Centre, Melur
=====================================================================

The website currently uses 4 REPRESENTATIVE images (not photos of the centre).
They are labelled as representative on the site. Replace them with real photos
of the Melur centre as soon as possible — real photos build far more trust.

All images live in:  src/assets/images/<folder>/
After adding a photo, point to it in ONE place:  src/config/images.ts
(Gallery entries are listed in src/data/gallery.ts.)

Any size or orientation works — every frame crops/fits automatically and keeps
faces in view using the "focus" setting in src/config/images.ts
(e.g. focus: "50% 30%" keeps the upper part of the photo visible).

Formats accepted: JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, SVG (logo), GIF (animations).
Tip: phone photos are fine. Shoot in daylight, landscape, uncluttered.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
A. ESSENTIAL (5 images)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. logo.svg  (or logo.png, transparent background)
   Folder:   src/assets/images/brand/
   Contains: The centre's official logo / emblem, if one exists.
   Size:     SVG preferred; PNG at least 512 x 512 px.
   Used in:  Header, footer, browser tab icon.
   Note:     Today a custom seal mark is shown. Only use the Alagappa University
             emblem if the university permits the centre to use it.

2. hero-main.jpg
   Folder:   src/assets/images/hero/
   Contains: Learners or staff inside the Melur centre (with consent), or the
             centre's welcoming reception area.
   Size:     1600 x 2000 px or larger.   Ratio: 4:5 portrait (any works).
   Used in:  Home page — arch-shaped photo in the top section.

3. centre-front.jpg
   Folder:   src/assets/images/about/
   Contains: Front of Karthik Complex with the centre's signboard visible.
   Size:     1600 x 1200 px.   Ratio: 4:3.
   Used in:  About page main photo + helps visitors recognise the building.

4. about-guidance.jpg
   Folder:   src/assets/images/about/
   Contains: A staff member guiding a learner at the desk (with consent).
   Size:     1500 x 1200 px.   Ratio: 5:4.
   Used in:  Home page — "Why learners choose Melur" section.

5. gallery-01.jpg
   Folder:   src/assets/images/gallery/
   Contains: Inside the centre — study/help desk area.
   Size:     1200 x 900 px.   Ratio: 4:3.
   Used in:  About page gallery.
